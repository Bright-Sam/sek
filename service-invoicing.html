Our Services

Invoicing & Billing Management

Turn completed loads into accurate, organized invoices and keep payment follow-up from becoming a bottleneck.

SEK helps bring structure to the billing side of your operation, from invoice preparation and submission through documentation checks and payment follow-up.

What we do

Practical support for the work behind the load.

We focus on the recurring coordination and administrative work that can slow a freight business down when it is handled inconsistently. Our role is to bring structure, visibility and follow-through to the process.

  • Invoice preparation and processing
  • Billing documentation checks
  • Invoice submission coordination
  • Payment-status follow-up
  • Missing-document follow-up
  • Billing records and reporting support
Who it helps

Designed for growing freight operations.

  • Carriers managing growing invoice volumes
  • Fleets that need consistent billing processes
  • Businesses experiencing billing delays or administrative overload
How it works

Support that fits your existing operation.

We start by understanding your current workflow, the volume you handle and where work is getting delayed or repeated. From there, we agree on the tasks SEK will manage, the information we need and the way updates should be communicated.

That means you get support built around your operation rather than a rigid package that creates more work.
