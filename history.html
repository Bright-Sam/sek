About — History

How SEK Legacy Services came to be

From a simple idea — that freight and finance should move in step — to a dedicated partner for carriers and fleets.

01
Founding

The Beginning

SEK Legacy Services was founded to solve a problem carriers know well: great freight operations were being let down by slow, inconsistent invoicing.

02
Early Growth

Building the Process

We developed a repeatable, five-step process connecting dispatch coordination directly to billing — cutting delays and errors on both ends.

03
Expansion

Serving More Fleets

As word spread among owner-operators and small fleets, we grew our team to support more accounts without losing the hands-on attention each one gets.

04
Today

A Full Back-Office Partner

SEK Legacy Services now supports carriers, fleets and brokers with dispatch, documentation, invoicing and reporting — all under one dependable roof.

Freight coordination and invoicing support for carriers, fleets and brokers who need their operations and their books handled with the same precision.
