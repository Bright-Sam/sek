Who We Are

Two disciplines. One dependable partner.

Freight only pays off when the paperwork is right behind it. SEK Legacy Services was built around that reality — pairing hands-on load coordination with disciplined invoicing and billing, so nothing slips through the gap between the road and the ledger.

We work as an extension of your back office: tracking loads, preparing and submitting invoices, chasing down documentation, and keeping your financial records clean and current. Carriers, owner-operators, fleets and freight brokers rely on us to handle the operational and administrative load so they can focus on the business itself.
